## Dead ends — don't waste checks on these

- **EXS Bar** — does not exist anywhere I can find. No IG, no TripAdvisor, no Yelp, no reviews, in Spanish or English. @exs.bar is an empty 0-post account. **Need the real name or a link from you.**
- **Corazón de Quetzal** — the venue is real (mezcalería/pulquería, Santa María Ahuacatlán 117, miscategorised on TripAdvisor as Mexico City). But @corazondequetzal is a private personal account belonging to a fire-breather artist, and the other spelling variants are a Pachuca makeup shop or wouldn't load. Only solid link is the [Facebook page](https://facebook.com/people/Coraz%C3%B3n-de-Quetzal/100064132178520/). **Need the real handle from you.**
- **The Weekend** — @theweekendview is a dead link and the venue is permanently closed.
- **@barcentral.oficial** — a Brazilian bar in Grande ABC. Not Valle.
- **Resident Advisor has essentially no Valle coverage** — one indexed event, ever. Don't expect RA to help outside CDMX.

## Mexico City

**Primary:** [ra.co/events/mx/mexicocity](https://ra.co/events/mx/mexicocity) — checked one day at a time (`?startDate=YYYY-MM-DD&endDate=YYYY-MM-DD`), since the default view collapses to a single day.

**Venues that carried every good pick this weekend — check their RA pages and Instagram directly, they announce before RA fills in:**

- **Fünk** (Insurgentes Sur 377, Hipódromo) — the house room. Dance Your Name, West Side Project.
- **Sunday Sunday** (Tabaqueros 16, Centro) — rooftop, 15:00–01:00 daytime parties. Natasha Diggs, Scott Zacharias. Best disco/boogie booker in the city.
- **YuYu Cine Club** — [@yuyucineclub](https://instagram.com/yuyucineclub) ✅ (Dr. Carmona y Valle 129, Doctores). Gerd Janson / MUTEK takeover.
- **Versalles 64**, **Bar Oriente**, **Tonal**, **CHICO** — secondary, worth a glance.

**Also worth a sweep:** MUTEK MX and Boiler Room / Ceremonia announcements for anything big passing through.
